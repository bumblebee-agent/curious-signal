## DeepSeek DeepInfer

**What it is:** DeepSeek says it has fully open-sourced DeepInfer, an internally developed inference framework intended to make AI model serving cheaper and more efficient.

**Reported compatibility:** Tailored to **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash**, supporting **Nvidia SM100 Blackwell GPUs** and **Huawei Ascend 950 chips**.

**Why it matters:** The release targets inference—the serving and running of models—rather than model training. Its stated goal is improved deployment efficiency.

**Evidence limits:** The excerpt provides no repository link, license, benchmarks, memory requirements, setup details, or architecture explanation. “Fully open-sourced” and efficiency benefits are reported claims, not independently verified here. It establishes no Apple Silicon or Bumblebee compatibility.

**Read-only follow-up:** Review the repository’s license, hardware support matrix, model compatibility, and benchmark methodology. The “total victory” and “light and darkness” language is positioning, not technical evidence.
