## Top Stories

### Show HN: Get your coding agent off ~2019 JavaScript, onto modern web primitives

Modern Web Guidance gives coding agents expert-curated web platform advice and browser compatibility data to help them choose native capabilities over legacy JavaScript patterns. Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox and MDN teams steward the project alongside the web development community. The project covers 250 web features and 203 developer use cases, including responsive layouts, accessible components, animation, performance, security and on-device AI. Its command-line tool searches and retrieves targeted guides locally using semantic search and BM25, without requiring API keys, and can scan codebases for legacy patterns. Modern Web Guidance uses Baseline compatibility data to tailor recommendations and favors lightweight fallbacks over heavy dependencies. Experimental Origin Trial APIs and volatile single-engine features remain searchable, but retrieving their guides requires explicit developer opt-in. The project reports substantial improvements in its November 8 automated evaluations: guided assertion pass rates rose from 58% to 94% for Antigravity, from 54% to 82% for Codex, and from 57% to 88% for Claude Code. The evaluation system combines realistic application tasks with static checks, browser behavior tests and accessibility assertions. Modern Web Guidance supports installation across several coding agents, including Codex, Claude Code and GitHub Copilot CLI. Google collects anonymous usage telemetry, including agent-generated search queries and retrieved guide IDs, but says it does not collect raw user prompts. Users can disable telemetry with the DISABLE_TELEMETRY environment variable. ([Show HN: Get your coding agent off ~2019 JavaScript, onto modern web primitives](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance)).

### Meta’s AI agent Muse lands on Apple Watch and Android Wear OS

Meta has launched its Muse AI assistant on Apple Watch and Android’s Wear OS, allowing users to interact through voice calls, text messages and dictation. The smartwatch expansion follows Muse’s September App Store debut and the introduction of a dedicated iPad app in October. Muse can connect to users’ accounts to manage email, meetings and bills, alongside tasks such as booking reservations, ordering groceries and making purchases. Meta recently introduced Spaces, which provide focused conversations where the agent works toward specific goals, and enabled U.S. users to video chat with customizable Muse avatars. The avatars respond with expressions and gestures, but Muse can hear users rather than see them. Muse surpassed 10 million U.S. downloads within its first 40 days on the App Store, becoming one of only 12 mobile apps to reach that milestone. ([Meta’s AI agent Muse lands on Apple Watch and Android Wear OS](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/metas-ai-agent-muse-lands-on-apple-watch-and-wear-os/)).

### ChatGPT is getting verified AI ‘Business Agents’

OpenAI is launching verified Business Agents in ChatGPT as part of a beta suite initially available to eligible US e-commerce and online service businesses. Users can chat with an agent when a participating business appears in a ChatGPT response, ask about its offerings and compare products. Businesses can supply instructions and context to guide their agents’ responses. OpenAI says Business Agent chats will be clearly labeled and separate from users’ main conversations. Business Hub will let companies claim and verify their organizations and view insights into mentions and discovery topics in ChatGPT. OpenAI has not disclosed specific verification methods. ([ChatGPT is getting verified AI ‘Business Agents’](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030552/openai-chatgpt-business-agents-hub)).

### Apple has new Mac app coming to make running local AI models easy

Leaker Aaron Perris has discovered evidence of Harbor, an Apple Mac app designed to run open-weight AI models locally on Apple silicon and turn Macs into private inference servers. Harbor is reportedly in private preview under Apple’s Claris brand. Harbor’s description says the app keeps data on users’ hardware, offers built-in model browsing and downloads, and automatically detects Macs connected over Thunderbolt 5 without manual configuration. Harbor also promises to pool unified memory across multiple compatible Macs, allowing clusters to run models larger than a single Mac can accommodate or handle more concurrent requests. The report provides no confirmed public release date. ([Apple has new Mac app coming to make running local AI models easy](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/10/apple-has-new-mac-app-coming-to-make-running-local-ai-models-easy/)).

## Tools and Smaller Signals

- [Pay for Hugging Face Hub with your Google Cloud Account](https://huggingface.co/blog/hub-gcp-marketplace): Hugging Face organizations can now pay for subscriptions, compute and storage through Google Cloud Marketplace, with charges appearing on their existing Google Cloud invoice instead of a credit card. The integration covers Team and Enterprise plans, Jobs, Inference Endpoints, Inference Providers, GPU Spaces, ZeroGPU quota extensions and additional storage at public Hugging Face prices. Organization administrators can subscribe through Google Cloud Marketplace, select a billing account and link an eligible Hugging Face organization. Hugging Face reviews each subscription before activating marketplace billing, and usage continues to use the organization’s existing payment method while approval is pending. Organizations with a credit card on file switch at the start of their next billing period after approval. Free-tier organizations without a card usually switch immediately after approval. Google Cloud replaces the organization’s credit card as its payment method, and organizations cannot add credits while marketplace billing is active. Team and Enterprise monthly included credits still apply before pay-as-you-go charges reach Google Cloud. Administrators can monitor usage in Hugging Face billing settings and charges in Google Cloud billing reports, while linking and unlinking actions appear in organization audit logs.
- [Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend](https://latent.space/p/resend/): Resend’s prominence in AI coding recommendations highlights how agents can influence developers’ choice of infrastructure. One study found that Claude Code ranked Resend first in 64 of 102 extracted email recommendations, while SendGrid ranked first seven times and appeared as an alternative 55 times. A separate, broader study found that Postmark led across most models. Resend co-founder and CEO Zeno Rocha said the company had 4.3 million users and 120,000 paying customers at the time of recording. Resend built early distribution through React Email, which let developers create templates and send them through competing providers, putting Resend’s code into projects before developers considered switching email services. Resend also offers a Markdown pricing table that agents can read without interacting with a slider. Resend’s MCP server had expanded from roughly 20 tools or fewer at launch to more than 80, with the aim of supporting dashboard functions through chat. Rocha’s inspection of oversized tool responses underscored that reducing tool counts alone does not solve context-window problems. Email delivery also presents trust and abuse challenges beyond winning an agent’s recommendation. Resend introduced custom tracking domains to address the risk that phishing activity on a shared tracking domain could damage legitimate customers’ links. Rocha said he wanted to preserve the free tier despite the risk of spammer sign-ups.
- [Helping caregivers use AI in everyday life](https://openai.com/index/helping-caregivers-use-ai-in-everyday-life): OpenAI Academy announced a free, in-person AI Skills Jam for Caregivers & Community Health for November 10 in Oakland, Tampa, Pittsburgh, Atlanta and Detroit. The event brings together OpenAI Academy, Caregiver Action Network, Alzheimer’s Association and American Diabetes Association during National Family Caregivers Month. The workshops aim to help caregivers and people living with chronic or serious health conditions use ChatGPT to organize schedules, simplify trusted educational materials, find community resources and prepare support group discussions. OpenAI facilitators and mentors provide hands-on guidance, and participants need no technical background. The training emphasizes protecting personal information, understanding AI’s limitations and consulting healthcare professionals when appropriate. AI tools should complement rather than replace medical, legal or financial advice. OpenAI estimates that more than 40 million weekly ChatGPT messages about healthcare in the United States concern care for someone else.
- [MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3-Image · Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3-Image): MiniMax’s MiniMax-H3-Image combines text-to-image generation and image editing in one checkpoint, supports up to 16 reference images, and produces roughly 16-megapixel images without an upscaler. The model uses six sampling steps without classifier-free guidance, with supported formats including 4096×4096 square images and 6272×2688 ultrawide images. MiniMax claims the model offers strong realism, detailed imagery and leading instruction following among open-source models. Local deployment requires substantial model storage: the image checkpoint is listed at 68.65 GB, while the separately downloaded text encoder adds 66.7 GB. An optional 17.9 GB prompt-expansion model converts short text-to-image prompts into detailed descriptions. Supported inference frameworks include SGLang, vLLM-Omni, Diffusers and ComfyUI, with NVIDIA and AMD accelerator configurations documented. MiniMax also offers hosted access through its API, Hailuo web apps and MiniMax desktop apps. The hosted API expands short prompts in any language into the detailed English descriptions expected by the image model. MiniMax-H3-Image and its optional prompt expander are governed by the MiniMax-H3-Image Community License Agreement, which includes a separate written-authorization provision.
- [MiniMax H3 Image is natively supported in ComfyUI on day-0](https://v.redd.it/8h0lmt1jct0i1): The post announces day-zero native support for MiniMax H3 Image in ComfyUI. One checkpoint handles text-to-image generation and multi-reference editing, sampling in six steps at guidance 1 without negative prompts. It supports native high-resolution output, including 4096×4096, and prompts up to 4096 tokens. Getting started requires updating ComfyUI, downloading the model, VAE and Qwen3-VL text encoder, and loading the supplied template; Comfy Cloud is an alternative. This is an announcement, not an unresolved support request.
- [MIT-led team receives funding to develop next-generation alternatives to modern weed killers](https://news.mit.edu/2026/mit-led-team-receives-funding-next-generation-alternatives-modern-weed-killers-1110): MIT researchers will lead an effort funded with up to $53 million from the U.S. Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health to develop more effective herbicides with lower risks to human health and the environment. The project, called Precision Learning for On-target Weed control, is led by Christopher Voigt, head of MIT’s Department of Biological Engineering, under the agency’s STREAM program. U.S. farmers use more than 1 billion pounds of herbicide annually, but resistant weeds are threatening the effectiveness of widely used products. Herbicide exposure may also contribute to cancer, metabolic and endocrine disorders, and other adverse health outcomes. The research team will develop generative artificial intelligence models to propose chemical compounds and small interfering RNA that disrupt essential plant enzymes. The models will seek candidates that are inexpensive to synthesize, less likely to induce resistance, and less likely to harm people or ecosystems. New alternatives could also reduce U.S. dependence on imported phosphorus used in glyphosate. The researchers plan to screen AI-generated candidates, feed experimental results back into the models, and evaluate promising molecules in greenhouses under different agricultural conditions. The first greenhouse tests of initial RNA interference candidates are expected to finish by the end of the program’s first year. The project will also develop living microbial systems to degrade agrochemicals before the chemicals reach food, soil, or waterways, with the aim of reducing environmental and human exposure.
- [PAN 2 Released](https://v.redd.it/oj0nh80o4v0i1): The PAN 2 release post describes an open-weight 29B latent diffusion model supporting video up to 20 seconds at 720p and 24 fps under Apache 2.0. The author says training code is also released, while vLLM availability is expected later that day. Its LLM generates dense scripts with timed events and actions, with camera control nested under events but separated from actions. The author reports successful informal tests of numerous timed camera moves and invites feedback; no troubleshooting issue or resolution is presented.
- [Toyota brand Scion is back and it's going to sell a kei truck in America](https://www.theautopian.com/scion-is-back-and-its-going-to-sell-a-freaking-kei-truck-in-america/): Toyota announced a revival of Scion as “Scion by Toyota,” a mobility “freedom lab” focused on experimental designs, vehicle concepts and features. Toyota originally launched the youth-oriented marque in 2003 and retired it in 2016 after years of declining sales. The first production vehicle, the KEI-01, is an ultra-compact utility truck based on the Toyota Pixis, itself a rebadged Daihatsu Hijet Jumbo Cab. The KEI-01 has a 660cc engine, a continuously variable transmission, four-wheel drive, a three-way folding cargo bed and a 600-pound capacity for passengers and cargo. Heating and air conditioning are standard. The KEI-01 meets American low-speed vehicle safety standards, with a top speed below 25 mph and use restricted to roads with posted limits below 35 mph. Toyota plans a limited fleet pilot beginning in late 2027, targeting commercial and agricultural applications rather than everyday driving. Wider availability will depend on performance and owner feedback. The revived lineup also includes the SxS Concept, a hybrid side-by-side off-roader unveiled at the 2025 SEMA Show. The SxS Concept combines a turbocharged Tacoma four-cylinder producing roughly 300 horsepower with a fully electric “Silent Mode,” but Toyota has no current plans for public availability. A separate compact outdoor personal-mobility concept features rugged tires and a portable design intended for narrow trails and transport in a car.
- [AI usage generates a fragile codebase](https://evanxmerz.com/post/ai-usage-generates-a-fragile-codebase/): An unnamed developer who was laid off from Meta in May argues that heavy reliance on AI coding tools can undermine the reliability and maintainability of large SaaS products. The developer speculates that resistance to AI adoption contributed to the layoff, while reporting that AI generated at least 90% of their code during their final months at Meta without doubling their output. The developer says another large technology employer now expects fully AI-generated code. The developer identifies four risks: shallow tests that miss meaningful user scenarios, unnecessary complexity, declining human understanding of codebases, and code production that outpaces careful review. The developer argues that line-level test coverage can conceal important gaps and that AI can solve narrowly scoped problems while overlooking longer-term architectural consequences. The developer describes rising incident counts, slower recovery, errors visible only to users, and deteriorating user experience as warning signs of fragile AI-generated software. The developer attributes these problems to inadequate testing, observability, review, and developer familiarity, although the account does not establish that AI caused the reported outcomes. The developer argues that high-quality software requires sustained human judgment about customer needs, architecture, testing, and maintenance rather than an emphasis on maximizing AI-generated output.
