## The News

The main reports are dated October 29, 2026.

Mistral’s weight release leads a day of deployable AI announcements, alongside scrutiny of model safety and AI search traffic.

First, from Hacker News: Mistral AI has released the weights of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk, a one-trillion-parameter, natively multimodal model trained from scratch on European infrastructure. The release follows a public preview and adds improvements in coding, defensive cybersecurity and reasoning efficiency. Mistral reports that the model scores 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, effectively tying GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens per task on average. Mistral Large 4 also reaches 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, up 13 percentage points from its preview while reducing output tokens by 30%. Developers can select low, medium or high reasoning modes to balance performance and cost.

Next, from Hacker News: Tinyworldformer uses two small neural networks to generate and play simplified games on a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W. A world model predicts the next frame and reward from the current frame and action, while an actor selects actions from the generated frames. Neither network uses the real game rules during play on the board. The project supports Breakout, Doom-lite, Flappy Bird, Pong and Pac-Man lite. Its world models contain 26,000–131,000 parameters and reuse a single transformer block across multiple passes to conserve memory. Quantization-aware training enables int8 computation, while embeddings, normalization and residual operations remain in float32.

Also, from r/comfyui: The post promotes agentY as a free, MIT-licensed AI agent inside ComfyUI for Windows and Apple Silicon. It claims support for local LLMs through Ollama or Strata, workflow creation using web sources and official templates, output review and re-rendering, Remotion-based video editing, MCP integrations, parallel agents, and Slack. This is a feature announcement, not a troubleshooting request; no issue or resolution is presented.

The author questions whether generated Alibaba storage and proxy URLs were verified as active and disputes framing unprivileged Linux user namespaces as suspicious. They connect the post's timing to Cohere's reported multibillion-dollar Series E announcement, including up to $1.6 billion from the Canadian government. This is the author's criticism, not an established finding of misconduct; they request confirmation about the URLs, and no resolution is supplied.

Across 18 sampled comments: The selected comments are largely distrustful of Cohere, interpreting its messaging about Chinese models as fearmongering or commercially motivated.

Worth noting, from Hacker News: Supports separate caption layouts for multiple screen shapes from one file. Overtone is an MIT-licensed captioning toolkit from Lont that creates separate caption layouts for horizontal, vertical or custom screen shapes from one file. A video.js plugin selects the cut that fits the player, and users can export each cut as WebVTT or SRT. Overtone’s layout rules avoid weak-word line endings, keep names and numbers together, separate speakers and turn pauses into readable breaks.

Another item, from TechCrunch AI: French AI company Mistral has released the weights for Mistral Large 4, a trillion-parameter model nicknamed “Le Chonk,” allowing businesses and governments to download and deploy the system on their own infrastructure. Mistral says ML4 matches the performance of open-weight rival GLM-5.3 while using 47 percent fewer tokens per task and leads open models on Artificial Analysis’ Coding Agent Index. Those performance and efficiency claims come from Mistral. Mistral is emphasizing cybersecurity and sovereign control, arguing that organizations can use ML4 to detect, analyze and respond to threats without depending on continued access to an outside provider.

Also in the mix, from TechCrunch AI: Granola launched free, unlimited dictation on Mac on October 29, expanding beyond its meeting-notetaking business. Users can press the Fn key to speak, and Granola cleans up and formats the speech into text across apps, including emails, messages, notes and AI prompts. Granola said iOS and Windows support will follow soon. The launch reflects growing convergence among voice AI apps, as Wispr added meeting notetaking months after establishing itself in dictation.

Shifting gears, from Hacker News: Trakkr Research found that just 40% of 1,285 statistics cited by five AI assistants could be traced to an original study supporting the same claim. The October 2026 investigation asked ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews 200 numerical questions, then followed citation trails up to five pages deep. Another 8% reached research whose figures or context differed from the answer, while 40% reached sources that could not be checked and 11% led to no research at all. ChatGPT had the highest share of supported statistics at 57% and quoted the fewest numbers, averaging 1.3 per answer.

One more, from The Verge: Reddit reported that October search traffic fell by more than 1 million referrals per week on average, as CEO Steve Huffman blamed part of the decline on Google’s AI-powered search features. Huffman said AI Mode does not drive traffic to Reddit and that AI Overviews and AI Mode push website links farther down search results. Huffman also said Reddit believes Google is ranking its machine-translated content lower, primarily affecting traffic outside the United States. Huffman argued that demand for Reddit remains strong, citing Google searches for Reddit and continued intensive crawling by Google. Reddit is seeking to become a direct daily destination through personalized recommendations, including a new “For You” feed, and experiments with AI-generated videos of posts.

Another update, from r/StableDiffusion: The LokkenJP/TenEros_Max_optimized_learned repository carries a sensitive-content warning stating that it may contain potentially harmful or sensitive information.

Looking ahead, from Hugging Face Blog: IBM has released Granite Switch 4.1.1, a backward-compatible patch to version 4.1 that requires no migration and leaves the base model unchanged. The modular architecture lets developers add specialized, cache-efficient adapters to a single model endpoint. The update introduces a Granite Speech ASR model supporting speech-to-text in 10 languages with native punctuation and capitalization. An updated Decision adapter in granitelib-core improves overall performance on the Jev Decision Index test set.
