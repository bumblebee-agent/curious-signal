Next, from Hacker News Top, report dated September 27, 2026: Fireworks Research has released Ember-1, a new specialized language model that delivers the same quality as Kimi K3 while using approximately 40% fewer tokens.

Built on the Kimi K3 architecture, Ember-1 was designed to reduce unnecessary reasoning traces without sacrificing accuracy, addressing the high costs associated with long reasoning in automated coding and agentic workloads.

The development team conducted more than 50 training experiments and over 200 evaluations using the Fireworks Serverless Training platform to create new algorithms that shorten reasoning processes.
