Next, from Hacker News: A former Microsoft Research leader announced the dissolution of the AI Frontiers sub-organization following its reorganization into Microsoft AI. The leader explained that AI Frontiers formed in 2023 to provide the organized, high-speed research support that individual efforts lacked after the rise of ChatGPT. The team focused on developing autonomous agent frameworks like Autogen and fundamental model capabilities such as the Full Bandwidth Transformer. They also established a shared monorepo to distribute their pretraining code and pipelines across Microsoft Research. The leader stated that the subsequent merger into Microsoft AI created a philosophical conflict between the research-focused culture of AI Frontiers and the scaling-only approach of the former Inflection AI leadership.

Across 18 sampled comments: The community views the shutdown of Microsoft Research's AI Frontiers group as a symptom of a broader industry shift away from fundamental, exploratory research toward short-term, transformer-centric scaling.

Also, from r/LocalLLaMA: Linellazatin pushed a commit to the llama.cpp repository that refines the Mixture of Experts cache implementation to improve CPU and device efficiency. The update introduces a command-line flag called moe-cache-cpu-share that allows the system to compute a defined fraction of cache misses on the CPU using host experts rather than uploading them to the device. This change modifies the computational graph to build the expert feed-forward network twice, once on the device from cached slots and once on the CPU, and then adds the two results before applying the down bias. The system now handles negative expert identifiers by skipping them in matrix multiplication operations and writing zero rows to maintain data integrity.

Across 18 sampled comments: The community views the pull request as a significant, thoughtful step forward for hybrid offloading in llama.cpp, particularly for handling missed experts on CPU.
